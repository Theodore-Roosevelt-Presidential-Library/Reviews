Visitor quotes widget

Four layouts, each adapting to whatever background it lands on. Every quote is a verbatim excerpt from a five-star review posted after opening day, chosen for whether it would persuade someone to make the drive. Pick a layout, copy the snippet.

Banner

One quote at a time, centred, rotating every eight seconds. For a full-width band between page sections — the most common use, and the one with the most breathing room. The block measures every quote on load and reserves the height of the tallest, so rotation never nudges the rest of the page; short quotes sit centred in that space rather than pinned to the top of it.

<div data-trpl-quotes data-layout="banner"></div>
<script src="https://reviews.labs.trlibrary.com/embed.js" async></script>

Card

The same rotation inside a bordered panel. Sits in a sidebar or a column without needing a background block of its own.

<div data-trpl-quotes data-layout="card" data-align="left"></div>

Wall

Three quotes at once, no rotation — motion in a grid is just noise. For a "what visitors say" section on the Visit or Plan Your Trip pages. Set data-count to change how many.

<div data-trpl-quotes data-layout="wall" data-count="3"></div>

Inline

A quiet rule-left quote for the flow of a page — a press page, a donor appeal, an itinerary. Smallest footprint of the four.

<div data-trpl-quotes data-layout="inline"></div>

On any colour block

The widget reads the background it was dropped onto and picks light or dark text from its luminance — white on anything dark, near-black on anything light. Stars are gold throughout, deepened on light backgrounds where bright gold falls below 3:1 against white. Nothing below is configured differently; all four are the same snippet.

On a photo or video background

The case that broke on the homepage. A Drupal section backed by a photograph or a video reports no background colour at all, so earlier builds looked past it, found the white page behind, and set near-black text on a dark picture. The widget now detects that an ancestor is painted with an image, gradient or full-bleed media and switches to white text with a soft shadow — the only choice that holds up over an image it cannot measure.

Detection is a default, not a lock, and it can only see what the page actually paints. On some Drupal sections nothing in the ancestor chain has a background at all — the colour comes from somewhere the widget can't reach — so it falls back to white and picks dark text. Say what you want instead: data-text="white" or data-text="dark". That attribute names the text, so there is nothing to read backwards.

Several blocks can sit on one page; the script only needs including once. Rotation pauses on hover, on keyboard focus, when the tab is hidden, when the block scrolls out of view, and entirely for visitors who have asked for reduced motion.
